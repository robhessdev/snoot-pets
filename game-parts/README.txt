GAME PARTS
==========
These files are the machinery behind the game. Look inside any time.
Each one starts with a note explaining what it does.

You don't need to change anything here yet. Your pet lives in
../my-embersnoot.js (one folder up).

  styles.css     the look: colours, spacing, layout
  checker.js     reads my-embersnoot.js and spots mistakes
  embersnoot.js  draws your pet with code, using your settings
  dewsnoot.js    plays Dewsnoot's painted animation frames
  battle.js      the battle rules
  sounds.js      sound effects, made with code (no sound files)
  notebook.js    the pages in your Notebook. Claude adds more over time.
  lessons.js     quests, the green/red bar, "show me the code"
  art/           Dewsnoot's artwork (a sprite sheet)
