SNOOT PETS — START HERE
=======================

1. PLAY
   Double-click  index.html  to open the game in your browser.

2. YOUR PET IS YOURS TO CHANGE
   Your fire pet's settings live in one file:  my-embersnoot.js
   Its name, your name and team, its victory line, its moves and its
   fire colours (sparks and glow) are all in there.
   Its body is painted art, so the file doesn't change its shape.

   The loop you'll use every time:
     change it  ->  save (Cmd+S)  ->  reload the game (Cmd+R)  ->  read the bar at the top
     GREEN = it worked.  RED = it tells you what to fix.

3. FOLLOW THE QUESTS
   They're under the arena in the game, in three tiers from easy to hard.
   Quest 2 helps you install VS Code, the editor you'll use to change things.
   Other files that are yours to change: my-lore.js, my-quests.js and the
   practice folder (the quests tell you when).

4. NEED HELP?
   Open guide.html, or ask Claude in the Code tab:
   "Fix my-embersnoot.js and explain what was wrong."

Dewsnoot's artwork was made in Codex/ChatGPT. The original package is in Documents/Codex.
