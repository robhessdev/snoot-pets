THE MOST IMPORTANT THING

Embersnoot is yours. Change it.

Your pet lives in one file: my-embersnoot.js. Its name, colours, shape and battle moves are all in there, written in plain words with notes beside each one.

The loop you'll use every time:

  1. Change something in my-embersnoot.js in VS Code.
  2. Save: ⌘S
  3. Reload the game in your browser: ⌘R
  4. Read the bar at the top. Green means it worked. Red says what's wrong and how to fix it.

Dewsnoot stays the same. He's the opponent, and he gets a little tougher each time you level up.

Everything you learn along the way collects in the 📓 Notebook button at the top of the game.

GETTING STARTED

How to open the game

  1. Open Finder, go to Documents, then Snoot-Pets.
  2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser.
  3. Follow the quests under the arena. The first one is just playing.

Nothing needs installing to play. The second quest sets up VS Code, which you'll use to change your pet.

.html (HTML): the structure
What's on the page: headings, buttons, the arena.
.css (CSS): the look
Colours, spacing and layout.

What happens when you click, how the battle works, and your pet's settings.

Your pet and Dewsnoot are animated two different ways. Embersnoot is drawn by code, so changing a number changes its shape. Dewsnoot is a painted picture, so changing him means making new art. That's why Embersnoot is the one you can shape.

SAVING

What gets saved, and where

Your changes to Embersnoot
Saved in my-embersnoot.js when you press ⌘S. They're kept for good, like any document.
Your quests, level and wins
Saved automatically in this browser on this computer. Another browser or device starts fresh. Start over at the bottom of the game wipes them.
Save points (backups)
When your pet has changed, the game reminds you after a spar or a quest to make a save point. Quest 8 sets up Git, which takes snapshots of the whole folder so you can always go back.
If you share the game
Everyone who opens a shared copy starts from the beginning with the original Embersnoot. Nothing they do changes yours. Your changes only appear in the shared copy if you ask Claude to update it.

WHEN THE BAR GOES RED

Mistakes are normal, and fixable

The game still runs: it uses a safe value for whatever's broken, and the red bar says where to look. The three usual causes:

  • Curly quotes “like this” instead of straight ones "like this". Retype the quote in VS Code.
  • A missing comma at the end of a line. Every item ends with ,
  • A deleted bracket } or ]. Press ⌘Z in VS Code to undo.

VS Code also draws a red squiggly line under the exact spot. Still stuck? Ask Claude: “Fix my-embersnoot.js and explain what was wrong.”

WORKING WITH CLAUDE

Asking for a change

  1. Open the Claude app and switch to the Code tab.
  2. Choose Documents → Snoot-Pets as the folder to work in.
  3. Say what you want in plain words.

A good request covers three things: what to change, what to leave alone, and how to explain it. For example:

“Give Embersnoot a new move called Ember Rain that hits for 11 with a 75 chance. Only change my-embersnoot.js. Then explain what you added, line by line, in plain words.”

Afterwards, open the file in VS Code and look at what changed. Reading Claude's changes is the fastest way to learn.

TINY GLOSSARY

Words you'll see

string
Words inside quotes: "Embersnoot". The game shows them.
number
A plain number, no quotes: 8. The game can do maths with it.
list
Things inside [ ], separated by commas, like your moves.
{ } block
A group of settings that belong together, like one move.
hex colour
A # and 6 letters/numbers: "#ff8a24" is orange.
comment
A line starting with //. A note for humans. The computer skips it.
reload
⌘R in the browser: makes the page read your files again.
